# Changelogs - v2.8.1

### ✨ *What's new*
- **Profiles** – First-run setup for profiles, per-profile watch history and lists, and PIN protection.
- **Playback** – Profile-aware playback preferences, player features that were missing, and Cinemeta episode handling.
- **Extensions** – Cinejoy mirror extension, with an on/off switch for each installed extension.
